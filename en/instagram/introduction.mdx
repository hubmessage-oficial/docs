## Overview

The **Instagram** channel connects a professional Instagram account to {projectName}, allowing you to send and receive **Direct** messages through the same API used by the other channels.

Here you will learn how to:

- Prepare your Instagram account for the integration
- Connect the channel through the Dashboard or SDK
- Understand the platform's features and limitations

## Requirements

<Steps>
  <Step title="A professional Instagram account">
    The account must be **professional** — Business or Creator. Personal accounts cannot be connected.

    You can switch account types for free in the Instagram app under **Settings → Account type**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Instagram Direct messaging enabled">
    In the Instagram app, enable **Settings → Messages → Allow access to messages**. Without this permission, the connection completes but no messages are received.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **You do not need a Facebook Page.** Unlike older integrations, this connection is made directly to the Instagram account.
</Note>

## What you can send

| Type | Instagram |
|---|---|
| Text, image, audio, and video | Yes |
| Document | PDF only |
| Sticker | Delivered as an image |
| Contact and location | Not available |
| Buttons | Coming soon |
| Templates | Not available |

See the details in [Features and limitations](/en/instagram/limitations), or the [comparison across all channels](/en/messages/introduction#available-types).
