## Overview

Returns the tenant's enabled template webhooks, scoped by the `Authorization` header — there is no cross-tenant access.

Authentication credentials and the signing `secret` are **not returned** for security reasons.

<Note>
  This endpoint requires the **ENTERPRISE** role on your account.
</Note>
