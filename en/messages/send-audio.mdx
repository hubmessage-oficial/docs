## Overview

Sends an audio message to a contact. The `content.type` field must be `AUDIO` and the audio is sent via URL in the `attachments` array.

<Note>
  This endpoint only works when the [conversation window](/en/conversation-window) is open (24 hours).
</Note>

Accepted formats: AAC, AMR, MP3, M4A, OGG, OPUS, WAV, FLAC (converted to OGG/Opus; AMR is passed through); max size **16 MB**. See all the details at [Media validations](/en/messages/media-validations).
