## Overview

Sends a sticker to a contact. The `content.type` field must be `STICKER` and the sticker is sent via URL in the `attachments` array. Use a URL for a WebP file.

<Info>
  On Instagram and Messenger, the sticker is delivered as an image message
  (`IMAGE`). On WhatsApp Business, it is sent as a sticker. Telegram sticker
  sending is not available yet.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Instagram may respond with `200` even when the format or type is not delivered.
  For this channel, use WebP and expect the message to arrive as an image.
</Warning>

<Note>
  This endpoint only works when the [conversation window](/en/conversation-window) is open (24 hours).
</Note>
