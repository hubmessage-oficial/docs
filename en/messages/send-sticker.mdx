## Overview

Sends a sticker to a contact. The `content.type` field must be `STICKER` and the sticker is sent via URL in the `attachments` array.

<Note>
  This endpoint only works when the [conversation window](/en/conversation-window) is open (24 hours).
</Note>

Accepted formats: WebP, PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP, TIFF (normalized to WebP 512×512); max size **100 KB** (static) or **500 KB** (animated). See all the details at [Media validations](/en/messages/media-validations).
