## Overview

Sends a document to a contact. The `content.type` field must be `DOCUMENT` and the document is sent by URL in the `attachments` array.

The `caption` and `name` fields are optional. WhatsApp Business and Telegram use these metadata fields; Instagram and Messenger send only the file URL and ignore them.

## Support by channel

- **WhatsApp Business** — supports documents.
- **Instagram** — supports PDF documents. Other formats may receive `200`, but they will not be delivered.
- **Messenger** — supports documents.
- **Telegram Bot** — supports documents.

<Note>
  This endpoint only works when the [conversation window](/en/conversation-window) is open (24 hours).
</Note>
