## Overview

This process is necessary if you're currently using another provider, since Meta only allows a single provider to be active for a given number at a time.

## Requirements

- **Business Portfolio (BM)**: you need to have a business portfolio set up with the WhatsApp number already linked
- **Number with another provider**: the number needs to be connected with another provider

## Step by step

### Step 1 - Disconnect the number from the provider

- You'll need to log in to the other provider and disconnect your number

### Step 2 - Access your Meta account

- Go to: [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/)

### Step 3 - Access the Portfolio settings

- Click the relevant Business Portfolio (BM)
- Click to access the BM's settings

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/migrar-1.png" alt="Access Business Portfolio settings" />
</Frame>

### Step 4 - Select the WhatsApp account

- Click **WhatsApp Accounts**
- Click the relevant account you want to remove the old provider from

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/migrar-2.png" alt="Select WhatsApp account" />
</Frame>

### Step 5 - Look for the "Partners" option

- After clicking the relevant account, click **Partners**

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/migrar-3.png" alt="Access the Partners tab" />
</Frame>

### Step 6 - Remove the partner's access

- Next to the provider you want to remove, click **Manage**

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/migrar-4.png" alt="Manage partner" />
</Frame>

- After that, a modal will open. Click **Remove Access**

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/migrar-5.png" alt="Remove partner access" />
</Frame>

### Step 7 - Connection in Hub Message

After removing the old provider's access, you'll now be able to connect through {projectName}.

<Note>
  Make sure the number has been properly disconnected from the previous provider before starting the connection in Hub Message, to avoid conflicts during the process.
</Note>
