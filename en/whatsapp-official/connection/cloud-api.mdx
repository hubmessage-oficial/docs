## Overview

The **Cloud API** lets you connect a number directly to the **WhatsApp Official API** through your Meta Business Account (Business Manager). This is the most traditional method for new numbers and gives you access to all the features available on the platform.

## Requirements

- **Business Portfolio (BM)**: you need to have a business portfolio set up (verified, if possible. This gives the BM more credibility with Meta)
- **Account administrator**: you'll need to have an administrator profile on the business account and on Facebook
- **The number must not be in the WhatsApp app**: to use this connection method, the number can't be active in the WhatsApp app. You'll need to disconnect it from the app first. The process between disconnecting and attempting a connection to the Official API through the Cloud API can take a few minutes
- **A number that can receive SMS or calls**: you need a number that can receive an SMS or a phone call, since Meta will send a verification code

## Adding a number through Meta

### Step 1 - Access the Business Portfolio (BM)

- Go to: [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/)
- Click the gear icon for the relevant Portfolio

  <img src="/images/cloud-api-1.png" alt="Access Business Portfolio" />
</Frame>

### Step 2 - Access the WABA (Business Account) screen

- Click **"WhatsApp Accounts"**

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cloud-api-2.png" alt="WhatsApp Accounts" />
</Frame>

### Step 3 - Add a new WABA and a new number

You can add a WABA directly through Meta.

- Click the **Add** button

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cloud-api-3.png" alt="Add button" />
</Frame>

- Click **Create a new WhatsApp Business account**

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cloud-api-4.png" alt="Create a new WhatsApp Business account" />
</Frame>

### Step 4 - Fill in the business information

- **Set up the WhatsApp display name**: it's important that it matches your business name and follows [Meta's guidelines](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/757569725593362)
- **Select a category** that matches your business (Automotive, Education, etc.)

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cloud-api-5.png" alt="Fill in business information" />
</Frame>

### Step 4 - Choose the number

You'll have two options to choose from:

- **Use a display name**: with this option, Meta creates a virtual number (which can't receive calls or SMS) and only the display name is shown to the customer on WhatsApp
- **Add a number**: with this option, you'll need to add a new number

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cloud-api-6.png" alt="Choose the number" />
</Frame>

### Step 5 - Verify a phone number

If you choose the **"Add a number"** option, you'll need to verify your phone.

- Choose **SMS** or **Call**

After that, your number will be validated and you'll be able to use it to connect.

<Note>
  It's important to set up a payment method in order to send templates.
</Note>
