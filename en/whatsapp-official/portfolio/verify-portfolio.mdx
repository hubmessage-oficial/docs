## Overview

After creating your **Business Portfolio**, you need to verify it to unlock all the features of the **WhatsApp Official API**. This guide walks you through the full verification process.

## Step 1: Go to the site

Go to: [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/)

After accessing the site in the relevant portfolio, go to the **WhatsApp Accounts** menu screen.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/verificar-port-1.png" alt="Access WhatsApp Accounts" />
</Frame>

## Step 2: Access the screen to start the review

Click **Security Center**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/verificar-port-2.png" alt="Security Center" />
</Frame>

## Step 3: Select the verification use case

Before starting verification, choose the **Set up a WhatsApp Business account** use case.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/verificar-port-3.png" alt="Select use case" />
</Frame>

## Step 4: Start verification

After choosing a use case, click **Start verification**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/verificar-port-4.png" alt="Start verification" />
</Frame>

After that, a modal will open. Click **Start**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/verificar-port-5.png" alt="Start verification modal" />
</Frame>

## Step 5: Fill in the business information

- Set which country the business is located in
- Set the business type (Corporation, sole proprietorship, partnership, etc.)
- Indicate whether your business has a legal registration
- For Brazilian businesses, provide a valid CNPJ
- Enter the business's legal name as registered
- Enter the business's valid address
- Enter a valid business contact phone number
- Enter the business's official website

## Step 6: If Meta can't find the business registration

If Meta can't find a registration for the business, it will ask you to upload documents proving the business exists. Before that, though, it will ask you to select a way to confirm your connection to the business.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/verificar-port-6.png" alt="Confirm connection to the business" />
</Frame>

## Step 7: Upload documents

You'll need to submit a document proving the business actually exists.

**Verify the business's legal name**: some documents are recommended: Articles of Incorporation, National Registry of Legal Entities (CNPJ) document, and Individual Micro-Entrepreneur (MEI) certificate.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/verificar-port-7.png" alt="Upload business documents" />
</Frame>

**Verify address or phone number**: it's important to submit a document that contains the business's legal name and phone number or address (it can't be expired).

## Step 8: Track your request

After going through all these steps requested by Meta, you'll need to track the progress of the verification. This can be done directly within the **Security Center** section.

- This process can take a few days
- If Meta has any pending items, it will send a notification with instructions on how to proceed (if your request is rejected, review the points Meta raised and try again)

<Note>
  **Important notes:**
  - Submit official documents that are legible and good quality, as this makes it easier for Meta to review
  - The business information needs to be correct and up to date
</Note>
