## How does billing work?

Meta charges for use of the **WhatsApp Official API** based on **delivered template messages**. Regular messages exchanged within the support window are not charged by Meta per conversation. Rates vary depending on the template category and the recipient's country.

## What generates a charge

There is typically a charge when an **approved template** is sent.

**Examples:**

- Order confirmation
- Billing/collection
- Reminder
- Support recovery
- Marketing campaigns

## What does not generate a charge

Once the customer replies and the window is open, Meta does not charge for regular messages sent during that period.

**Examples:**

- Text messages
- Chatbot replies
- Agent messages
- Media sent
- AI messages
- Utility templates
- Templates sent within a window opened from a CTWA campaign

<Note>
  To better understand when the support window is open or expired, see the page on the [24-hour window](/en/whatsapp-official/24h-window).
</Note>
