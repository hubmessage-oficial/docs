## Overview

Updates an existing template and resubmits it for Meta approval. The body is the same used when creating the template.

<Warning>
  When updating an approved template, it goes back to `PENDING` status until Meta reviews it again.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The `templateId` is obtained from the [List templates](/en/templates/list-templates) endpoint.
</Note>
